My background

I’m Cristian Angelo M. Sitjar, a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering graduate from Cavite State University – CCAT Campus. After graduation, I joined JRA Technologies Inc. as a Junior Software Engineer, where I completed an intensive Erlang bootcamp before moving into production work.

During my two years in software development, I worked on backend CRUD features, debugging, data handling, server-side logic, and production issues. I also collaborated with senior and junior teammates, participated in regular stand-ups, and later helped incoming trainees during their bootcamp.

I’m currently expanding beyond Erlang by building projects with NestJS, REST APIs, Flutter, Firebase/Firestore, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and Git. I’m interested in roles where I can keep growing as a Software Engineer while contributing practical solutions from day one.
